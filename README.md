## About Me

I graduated in Computer Engineering from ISEC (Information Systems track), completed four professional internships — including an international Erasmus+ experience in Málaga, Spain — and won the **Medal of Excellence** in Mobile Application Development at **WorldSkills Portugal 2023** (national level).

My background in industrial environments with CNC machinery instilled a deep discipline for precision and systematic architecture. Today, I channel that rigor into RAG architectures, data pipelines, analytical modeling, business process modeling (**BPMN / OML / ERP foundations**), and robust Python backend engineering. Most recently, I interned as an **AI Engineer at Meight**, working in the data team on Hybrid Search architectures (`pgvector`, dense semantic vectors & sparse BM25 retrieval) and automated customer onboarding systems.

> *“I build systems that connect enterprise data, business processes, and artificial intelligence. From analysis to impact.”*
